# Voice

*Doctrine — how the brand talks, everywhere. Claims here require H-class provenance. Per-channel mechanics (lengths, formats, hashtags, CTAs) live in [channel-styles.md](channel-styles.md); word-level rules (capitalization, banned words, product names) in [glossary.md](glossary.md). Omit `## Contested` until a real collision exists.*

## Voice attributes

*What goes here: 3–5 named attributes, each with a one-line definition and a do/don't pair.*

## Tone by context

*What goes here: how the voice flexes — educational vs. promotional vs. support vs. crisis.*

## Never

*What goes here: constructions, cliches, and postures the brand never uses. If an exec bans a specific word, the word-level ruling goes to [glossary.md](glossary.md#banned-words); record it here only if it reflects posture.*

## Exemplars

*What goes here: 3–5 short passages of on-voice writing, each with a line on why it works. A designated evidence section (SPEC §17.3(b)): an exemplar keeps its source's provenance class, and a borrowed passage is not a decision about our voice.*
