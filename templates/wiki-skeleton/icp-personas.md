# ICP and personas

*Doctrine — the ICP is a decision, not a pattern-read. Claims below require H-class provenance; call-mined patterns enter as `inferred` annotations with an open question to ratify (SPEC §8). Omit `## Contested` until a real collision exists.*

## ICP

*What goes here: the firmographic definition — segment, size, industry, stack signals, disqualifiers. Claims here require H-class provenance. The current customer LIST is state → [customers.md](customers.md).*

## Anti-ICP

*What goes here: who we actively avoid and why — churn history, support cost, mission mismatch. Claims here require H-class provenance.*

## Personas

*What goes here: one subsection per persona — role, pains, triggers, objections, what convinces them. Fan out to `references/persona-<name>.md` when a persona outgrows one screen. **Exactly one persona carries a `primary: true` marker**, or this section carries a per-channel default map. Without it a consumer writing one asset has to rank the personas itself. Persona-targeted channel tactics live in [growth.md](growth.md).*

## Channel personas

*What goes here, for orgs selling through distributors, resellers, or platforms: the partner rep as an audience with their own pains, incentives, and objections — marketed **to** in order to sell **through**. Mark the section `<!-- not a buyer: we market THROUGH them -->` and point fan-outs at [partners.md](partners.md). Omit this section where no channel motion exists.*

## Customer language

*What goes here: verbatim phrases customers use for their problem and our solution, mined from calls and reviews — each phrase cites its source and keeps that source's provenance class. A designated evidence section (SPEC §17.3(b)). This section holds phrases-as-evidence; the ruling on which terms WE use lives in [glossary.md](glossary.md).*
