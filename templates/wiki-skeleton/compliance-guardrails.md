# Compliance guardrails

*Doctrine — the strictest file in the wiki, and prohibitions only: EVERY content task reads this file. Claims here require H-class provenance. Approved claims live in [business-core.md](business-core.md); keeping this file negative-space-only is what keeps its signal sharp. Ask the origin story of every hard prohibition.*

## Legal constraints on go-to-market

*What goes here: agreements that constrain who may be sold to or marketed to — territorial rights, rights of first refusal, exclusivity, OEM and white-label obligations, service-coverage limits, NDA'd relationships. Read first: these make an otherwise excellent campaign unlawful rather than merely off-brand.*

## Approval workflow

*What goes here: who approves what, on what turnaround, in what sequence, and where a draft waits. Include the rejection rate if it is known. Drafting freezes (the `blocked` marker) live here or beside the asset. Operational detail fans out to `references/compliance-review-workflow.md`.*

## Banned claims

*What goes here: claims we must not make — unsubstantiated superlatives, forbidden comparisons — each with why. Claims here require H-class provenance.*

## Regulated constraints

*What goes here: industry rules that bind copy — financial-promotion rules, health claims, data-residency promises, and the like.*

## Competitor conduct

*What goes here: disparagement policy, naming policy, comparison rules. Competitor facts live in [competitors.md](competitors.md); this section is only how we CONDUCT ourselves about them.*

## Trademark and naming

*What goes here: our marks, their marks, usage rules. Exact renderings of product names live in [glossary.md](glossary.md); this section is the legal usage rules.*

## Embargoes and timing

*What goes here: what cannot be discussed publicly yet — every entry carries an expiry date and H-class clearance provenance.*
