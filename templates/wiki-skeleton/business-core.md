# Business core

*Doctrine — decisions the org has made. Every claim below requires H-class provenance (`interview:<person>`, an archived human-authored document `<docs-source-id>:<run-id>/<file>`, or a principal's ruling captured in a channel); other evidence may only annotate, in `## Contested` (SPEC §8). Omit `## Contested` until a real collision exists.*

## Company facts

*What goes here: founding, ownership and capital structure, headcount, geography and languages, decision horizon. Flat premises everything else is downstream of. A consumer answering "who are these people" should not have to infer this from Positioning.*

## Product

*What goes here: what the product is, in customer language, and the 2–3 jobs it does. One claim per statement, each tagged, with a `^topic-key` on anything agents will cite. An org with several lines or services gives each a `### <line>` subsection. Claims here require H-class provenance.*

## Positioning

*What goes here: category, the for-whom statement, differentiated value, and the one positioning sentence agents build on. Persona-specific value props live in [icp-personas.md](icp-personas.md); strategy and channel bets in [growth.md](growth.md).*

## Right to win

*What goes here: the honest structural advantages — and their limits. H-class only; agent pattern-reads about why we win go to `## Contested` or [open-questions.md](open-questions.md) until a human ratifies them.*

## Pricing

<!-- if pricing is complex, fan out to references/pricing.md -->

*What goes here: model, tiers, floors, discount policy — and what agents may say about price. Carry the actual numbers, not only the permissions. Claims here require H-class provenance.*

## Approved claims

*What goes here: the exact claims agents may make, one per entry, each with its substantiation and a `^claim-<key>` topic key. Name which one is the **lead claim** (the sentence that heads a capability announcement) so a consumer does not have to rank them. Claims we must NOT make live in [compliance-guardrails.md](compliance-guardrails.md), not here.*

## Sales motion facts

*What goes here: cycle length, ACV bands, expansion motion — the sales-shape facts copy depends on, as ratified by a human. This section is illustrative: an org with no sales motion omits it rather than inventing one. CRM evidence that disagrees goes to `## Contested` with an open question, not into these claims.*
