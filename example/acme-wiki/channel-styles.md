# Channel styles

Voice is defined in [voice.md](voice.md); this file assumes it and adds channel mechanics. Which channels get investment and why is strategy → [growth.md](growth.md#channel-bets).

## LinkedIn

The flagship organic channel is Sam Whitfield's founder account; the company page reposts and never originates.
[confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^li-founder-first

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 120–220 words, one idea per post |
| Structure | claim in line one, evidence in the middle; no hook theatrics |
| Links | none in the post body; one link in the first comment when needed |
| Hashtags | none |
| Cadence | Sam Whitfield 3×/week, company page 2×/week |
| Engagement | reply within 24h on Sam Whitfield's posts; never engage Attribia threads ([compliance-guardrails.md](compliance-guardrails.md#competitor-conduct)) |

[confirmed | interview:sam-whitfield | 2026-07-16] ^li-mechanics

## Blog

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 1,200–2,000 words; cut before padding |
| Structure | answer first — the conclusion inside the first 100 words — method second, product last |
| SEO | one target query per post; the title states the answer, not the question |
| Internal links | every post links at least two cataloged assets; product mentions link to feature pages |
| Bylines | humans only — agents draft, a named human owns every post |
| Cadence / rate limit | one long-form post per week; never two in a week to fill a calendar |

[confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^blog-mechanics

## Email

Outbound to named accounts:

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | under 120 words, one CTA |
| Subject | specific and boring beats clever: "attribution for {{company}}'s 4 paid channels" |
| Sequence | 4 touches maximum, then out; re-entry needs a new trigger |
| Personalization | only fields the runbook trusts ([crm.md](crm.md)) plus publicly stated facts — nothing mined from call recordings |
| Sign-off | sender's real name and role; no image signatures |
| Cadence / rate limit | 4-touch sequence then out; re-entry needs a new trigger |

[confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^outbound-mechanics

Enterprise-tier outreach leads with SOC 2 Type II in the first touch — always, not when space allows.
[confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-08-05] ^email-soc2-first-touch

Newsletter: monthly, teaches one attribution method per issue, product news in a footer section only. Same subject-line rule as outbound.
[confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^newsletter-mechanics

## Web

- The homepage H1 is the positioning sentence ([business-core.md](business-core.md#positioning-sentence)), shortened — never a slogan invented per campaign. [confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^web-h1-rule
- CTA language: "See it on your data" (primary), "Talk to us" (secondary). Never "Book a demo" — the demo *is* the prospect's own data, and the CTA should say so. [confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^web-cta-language
- Every page carries at most one approved claim from [business-core.md](business-core.md#approved-claims), with its number. [confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^web-one-claim-rule
- Cadence / rate limit: none — pages ship when Dana Okafor signs them, not on a calendar. [confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^web-cadence

## Paid

| Platform | Constraints |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn Ads | headlines ≤ 8 words; approved claims only; no competitor names in ad copy |
| Google Ads | search only — branded plus high-intent category terms; state the price when the query implies evaluation |
| Cadence / rate limit | search is always-on; LinkedIn Ads only while a named campaign is live, never as a standing brand presence |

[confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^paid-constraints

Never bid on competitor brand terms. This is a standing decision, not a budget question.
[confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^no-competitor-bidding

## Channels declared absent

- **X / Twitter.** We do not post. Founder-brand lives on LinkedIn; a second network would split the one writing-time budget that actually works. [confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^absent-x
- **Community / forum.** No company-hosted server. Support is email and docs; we do not staff a Discord or Slack community. [confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^absent-community
- **Print / catalog.** Not a motion. [confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^absent-print
- **Field booths.** We speak at other people's events; we do not buy booths. [confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^absent-booths
