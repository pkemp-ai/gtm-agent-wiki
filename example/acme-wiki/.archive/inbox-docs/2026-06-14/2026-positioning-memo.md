# Positioning memo — June 2026

Author: Dana Okafor. Status: agreed with Sam, 2026-06-13.

## The category

We are a **revenue attribution** platform. Not marketing analytics. Not marketing
mix modeling. The narrower word costs us some search volume and buys us a clear
answer to "what is this", which we need more.

## Who we are for

B2B SaaS marketing teams, 100–1,000 employees, on HubSpot or Salesforce, running
three or more paid channels. Big enough that attribution is a real problem, too
small to hire a data team to solve it.

## The sentence

> For B2B SaaS marketing teams on HubSpot or Salesforce, Acme Analytics shows which
> marketing actually creates pipeline — multi-touch attribution live in days,
> without a data team.

## The competitive frame

MetricFlow sells the same promise to enterprises with six-week implementations.
DashForge sells a cheaper, shallower version to SMBs. Attribia sells the AI story.
We own the middle.

## Pricing

Growth $1,200/mo, monthly or annual, up to 8 users and 5 data sources.
Scale $3,000/mo, annual only, unlimited users and sources, Model Lab, SSO.
Enterprise custom, annual only.

List prices are public. Discounting is a sales conversation and never a marketing
claim.
