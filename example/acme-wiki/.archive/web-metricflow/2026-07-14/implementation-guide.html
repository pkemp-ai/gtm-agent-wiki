Your MetricFlow rollout, week by week

Every MetricFlow deployment is delivered as a guided six-week rollout led by a named implementation architect.

  1. Weeks 1–2 — Discovery. Data audit, warehouse review, model workshop.
  2. Weeks 3–4 — Build. Model configuration by your architect against your schema.
  3. Week 5 — Validation. Reconciliation against your existing reporting.
  4. Week 6 — Handover. Enablement for your analytics team.

Customers with an in-house analytics function typically complete discovery faster.
