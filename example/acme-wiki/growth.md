# Growth

Pipeline *results* live in [pipeline.md](pipeline.md). This file records the bets, not the scoreboard.

## Model

Two motions, deliberately only two: content-led inbound, and outbound to a named-account list. No PLG motion — the product needs a CRM connection before it shows value, so there is no meaningful self-serve moment to build on.
[confirmed | doc:2026-h2-growth-plan.md | 2026-07-14] ^growth-model

Founder-brand LinkedIn (Sam Whitfield's account) is the flagship organic channel and feeds both motions: it warms the named-account list and compounds inbound.
[confirmed | interview:dana-okafor | 2026-07-16] ^founder-brand-flagship

## Channel bets

Ranked by current investment. Verdicts are Dana Okafor's quarterly calls, revised on evidence — not running metrics.

| # | Channel | Thesis | Verdict (Q3 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Founder LinkedIn | Attribution is a trust sale; a founder explaining models beats a brand explaining features | Working — protect Sam Whitfield's writing time |
| 2 | SEO / blog | "How do I…" attribution queries are high-intent and underserved by vendors afraid to give the method away | Working — compounding |
| 3 | Outbound email | Named accounts plus a real trigger beat volume; hard 4-touch cap | Working when triggered, dead when not |
| 4 | LinkedIn Ads | Distribution for proof assets; retargeting content-warmed accounts | Paying for itself, no more |
| 5 | Google Ads | Catch category demand we didn't create; branded and high-intent terms only | Under review — decision due with Q3 numbers |

## Target accounts

- Definition: the ICP filter ([icp-personas.md](icp-personas.md#icp-definition)) plus a live trigger — new CMO or VP Marketing, HubSpot install detected, 3+ paid channels visible, or a competitor contract likely up. [confirmed | interview:priya-shah | 2026-07-20] ^target-account-definition
- The list holds 400 accounts, refreshed monthly by marketing ops. Nobody adds accounts ad hoc — a candidate goes to ops with its trigger named. [confirmed | interview:morgan-lee | 2026-07-30] ^target-list-discipline
- The list itself is CRM data; how to pull it is in [crm.md](crm.md).
